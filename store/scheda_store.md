**Full description**

The ball climbs on its own. You have one control: tap, and it turns 90°.

Turn inside the corner: too early and you fall off the side, too late and you fly off the end. Every platform is a point. Turn right in the middle of the corner for a PERFECT: bonus point, the ball snaps to the centre and your combo climbs the scale.

Platforms get narrower, the ball gets faster, colours shift. When you miss, you see exactly where and why, and you're back with one tap.

Two more modes, still one tap: TOWER, where you climb a spiral round a spinning tower and jump the holes in the ramp, and DINO, where you run and jump cacti and birds like in the browser's offline game. DINO opens when you play three days in a row.

And to play together, AIR HOCKEY: create a lobby on the same Wi-Fi or online, send friends the code, split into teams, 1 vs 1 or 2 vs 2, and drag your mallet. If someone's missing, the CPU plays.
